Privacy Policy — VCH EchoCalc iOS App

Last updated: 11 August 2026

VetCardioHub ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have under data-protection laws including the UK GDPR and EU GDPR. It covers the VetCardioHub (VCH) EchoCalc app and, separately, our website and mailing list.

In one line: the app sends us nothing. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no clinical data transmission of any kind.

1. The VCH EchoCalc app

Everything you enter into VCH EchoCalc — measurements, patient and owner names, breed, study dates, clinical comments, saved studies, generated reports, and any practice logo or signature image you add — is stored only on your own device by the app. It is never transmitted to, collected by, or accessible to us, and it is not held on any server we operate.

  • Because this data never leaves your device, we cannot see it, recover it, or restore it for you.

  • Deleting the app, or losing, resetting or replacing your device, will permanently and irrecoverably delete your saved studies (except to the extent they are captured in your device’s own encrypted backups).

  • The app has no account system and no cloud sync. There is nothing to log in to, and nothing syncs between devices.

If we ever introduce a feature that transmits or stores study data off your device, we will update this policy and tell you before you use it.

2. Patient and client data — you are the controller

VCH EchoCalc is a professional tool, and the records you create in it may contain personal data relating to your clients (for example an owner’s name) as well as patient details. Because that information never reaches us, you and your practice are the data controller for it, and we are not your processor.

You are responsible for handling it in line with your own professional and data-protection obligations — including whether clinical records may be held on the device you are using, how that device is secured, and how long records are kept. Where a patient’s record belongs in your practice management system, export it there rather than relying on the app as a record store.

3. Reference-data downloads

To keep the built-in glossary current, the app periodically downloads a reference file from vetcardiohub.com. This request:

  • sends us no personal or clinical data about you, your practice or any patient — it is a plain read-only fetch with no request body and no identifiers;

  • is used only to update the reference text shown in the app; and

  • is not used to track you. If it is unavailable, the app keeps working from its bundled copy.

As with any web request, our hosting provider’s ordinary server logs may record the originating IP address and time for security purposes. Those logs are not linked to you, to any patient, or to anything you have entered in the app.

4. Photographs you add

If you add a practice logo or a signature image so that it appears on exported PDF reports, that image is copied into the app’s own storage on your device. The app requests access only to the image you choose; it does not browse, index or upload your photo library, and the image is never transmitted to us. You can replace or remove it at any time from within the app.

5. On-device storage and permissions

The app uses your device’s own storage to save studies and settings. This is strictly necessary for the app to function, does not track you, and transmits nothing to us. The app does not use advertising identifiers, does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not request access to contacts, location, health data, the camera or the microphone.

6. Information we collect (website & mailing list)

Separately from the app, when you interact with our website we may collect:

  • Email address — when you sign up to receive our clinical resources, tutorial emails or other updates.

  • Optional details — if you volunteer information such as your practice type or professional interests.

  • Technical information — Brevo (our email provider) records your IP address, browser type, and the date/time of signup for security and consent records.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. None of this is connected to your use of the app; we cannot tell whether a subscriber uses VCH EchoCalc.

7. How we use your information

  • Send you the resources you requested.

  • Deliver a short series of follow-up emails to help you use them.

  • Send occasional updates or new resources only if you have given consent.

  • Maintain accurate subscriber records and comply with legal obligations.

8. Legal basis for processing

Under data-protection law, we rely on:

  • Consent — when you opt in to receive updates or newsletters.

  • Legitimate interest / performance of your request — to send the resource and follow-up emails you specifically asked for.

9. Sharing your information

We use trusted third-party services to operate our website and email list:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue) — email marketing and automation platform.

  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security provider.

These providers act as our data processors and only process your information on our instructions. We do not sell or trade your personal data. These services relate to the website and mailing list only — your app data, as described in sections 1 and 2, is never shared because it never leaves your device.

10. Data retention

We keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed or until you ask us to delete it. Unsubscribing stops all future messages, though Brevo may retain minimal records to demonstrate compliance with legal obligations. App data is retained only on your own device, under your control, for as long as you choose to keep it.

11. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.

  • Request correction or deletion of your data.

  • Withdraw consent at any time (unsubscribe link in every email).

  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or your local data-protection authority if you are elsewhere.

These rights apply to the data we actually hold — that is, website and mailing-list data. We cannot action a request in respect of data held only on your device, because we have no access to it.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@vetcardiohub.com.

12. International data transfers

Brevo’s servers may be located outside the UK/EU. Where data is transferred internationally, appropriate safeguards (such as adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses) are applied to protect it.

13. Security

For our website and mailing systems, we use encryption and secure, reputable providers to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Your app data is not transmitted at all — its security depends on the security of your own device, so device passcode, encryption and screen lock matter, particularly where records identify clients.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this policy or your personal data, contact VetCardioHub at privacy@vetcardiohub.com.
