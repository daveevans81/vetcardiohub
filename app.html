Already using it in your browser?

The iPhone app keeps its own records, so your existing entries do not appear automatically. Moving them across takes about a minute.

  1. In the browser version, go to More → Data & Export and choose Export Backup.
  2. Install the app from the App Store and open it.
  3. On the welcome screen, choose Import patient(s) from backup and pick the file you just saved.

Keep the backup file somewhere safe afterwards. It is the only copy of your history outside the app.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no charge, no subscription, no advertising and no in-app purchase. The project is funded by voluntary support.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. Android and desktop users should use the browser version, which has the same features. On Android you can add it to your home screen and it will behave much like an installed app, including offline breath counting.

Where is my pet's data stored?

On your device only. Nothing is sent to VetCardioHub or to any third party. That also means the records are not backed up for you — export a backup periodically, and keep it somewhere safe.

Does this replace veterinary advice?

No. It is a monitoring and record-keeping tool that helps you and your veterinary surgeon see patterns clearly. It does not diagnose, and it is not a substitute for professional assessment. If your pet's breathing rate or effort rises, or you are worried, contact your veterinary practice.

What is a sleeping respiratory rate, and why does it matter?

It is the number of breaths your pet takes per minute while genuinely asleep. In dogs and cats with heart disease it is one of the earliest and most reliable signs that fluid is beginning to accumulate in the lungs, often rising before any outward change you would notice. Our monitoring guide explains how to measure it properly.
