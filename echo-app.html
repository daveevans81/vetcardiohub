VCH EchoCalc for iPhone & iPad

Enter a study, get every scaled index against its published reference interval. Built for use at the scanner, where the measurements are taken.

Download on the App Store Or use the web calculator

Requires iOS 17 or later. On Android or desktop, the web calculator has the same reference models.

  • Allometric scaling. Normalised dimensions against published reference intervals, with the model stated rather than assumed.
  • Breed-specific normals. Where breed data exist, they are used in preference to a generic canine model.
  • MINE prognostic score. Derived and validated against survival in a published MMVD cohort.
  • Diastolic grading and Qp:Qs. With the interpretive caveats shown alongside the grade, not buried.
  • Works offline. No connection needed in the echo room, and nothing leaves the device.
  • Written for clinicians. Citations and reference sources are shown, so you can check the basis of any number.
